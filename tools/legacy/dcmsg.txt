i9100: scrub EPDIS/SNAK/STALL out of every endpoint arm

Both EP0 halves and the EP1 probe all read back EPENA-rejected
(0x00028000 = USBACTEP|NAKSTS) with an EPDISBLD in diepint. The init
disable loop stamped DXEPCTL_EPDIS with an absolute write; on a
never-enabled endpoint bit30 can stay latched, and every |= arm then
writes EPDIS|EPENA together - the endpoint cancels its own arm.
Only stamp EPDIS when the ep was actually enabled, and mask
EPDIS|SNAK|STALL out of every arm on EP0-IN/OUT and EP1.
